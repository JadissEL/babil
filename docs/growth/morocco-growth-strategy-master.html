1. Executive summary

Become Morocco’s default honest layer on visas, studies abroad, and travel prep — replacing rumor with sourced, Morocco-specific clarity while earning through aligned affiliates and partners.

North-star levers

Trust Disclaimers, freshness, official links — never guarantee outcomes.
Distribution SEO + TikTok/Reels + student depth + QR from offline.
Monetization Flights, insurance, tests, remittance — contextual only.

2. Morocco market snapshot

  • Mobile-first discovery; peak anxiety before TLS/consulate and exam results.
  • French for formal search and education; Darija/MSA hooks for video — always subtitle FR.
  • Trust gap: scams and exaggerated TikTok claims — differentiate with institutional tone + transparency.
  • Social: Facebook groups for “avis”; short video for explainers; Google for troubleshooting.

5. Phased roadmap

Phase 1 · 0–3 mo

Goals: Channel-product fit; 10k–50k MAU band; affiliate baseline.

Actions: 15 SEO spokes; 3 TikTok/wk; small Google; 2 lycées + 2 univ; 10 ambassadors.

Budget: L 25k–50k · M 60k–120k · H 150k–300k MAD/mo

Goals: National student recognition; partnership pipeline; SEO moat.

Actions: Video factory; scale Meta/TikTok; 5–10 city tour; 50–150 ambassadors; B2B deck.

Budget: L 40k–80k · M 100k–200k · H 250k–450k MAD/mo

Goals: Category leadership; flagship institutional win; organic majority.

Actions: Reports & conferences; airline/OTA depth; diaspora SEO.

Budget: M 150k–350k · H 500k+ MAD/mo

8. Execution templates (sections 13–15 · v1.2)

UTM & naming

utm_source (google, meta, tiktok, offline) · utm_medium (cpc, paid_social, qr) · utm_campaign (e.g. 2026-06_schengen_ma) · utm_content (creative id).

Offline QR: utm_source=offline&utm_medium=qr. Ambassadors: utm_content=amb_slug. Lowercase ASCII, underscores.

Influencer / ambassador brief
  • Deliverables: 1 Reel or TikTok + stories or 1 Short; FR subtitles if Darija.
  • No “visa garanti”; no impersonation; #publicité in caption.
  • Pinned comment: one official link. Contract includes creative guardrails.
90 min workshop runbook

Prep: UTM QR, printed checklist, consent forms, deck legal review.

Flow: 10 min disclaimers → 25 min official sources → 20 min erreurs fréquentes → 20 min Q&A → 15 min signup/sponsors (labeled).

Post: thank-you + recap; log questions → content backlog.

Final recommendation

80% effort: SEO + systematized short video + ambassadors + contextual affiliate + 2–3 city offline proof.

Avoid early: visa outcome promises; spam in groups; wide PMax without maturity; chasing logos without pilot data.
